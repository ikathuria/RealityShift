  1. A clockA turn counter and countdown give the world a heartbeat, so there's always a reason to check back.
  2. Stakes and scoreRank, influence and a streak give you a way to measure yourself against other players.
  3. Change, not just stateArrows show movement since last turn, and the yellow tick marks where each bar started.
  4. CharactersA quote from the AI agent currently running the country gives you someone to overthrow.
  5. Things happeningEvents pop up in a feed, and rivals are pinned on the map, so the world feels alive while you watch.
  6. A juicier buttonThe main button presses down with a chunky shadow, and the label says what you're actually doing.
